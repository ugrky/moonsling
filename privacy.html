Moonsling — privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · app version 1.0

Moonsling is made by Ugur Kaya, an independent developer. This policy explains what the iOS app does with your data. In short: it does not collect any.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, stardust and cosmetics are saved in a file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics services, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

The daily board

The other names on the daily board are computer-controlled pilots (marked "bot"). They are simulated on your device from the day's date. There is no online play and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, iOS asks for your permission, and the reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved. You can turn it off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads, purchases or online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
