Moonsling — support

Hold to orbit. Let go to fly. Questions, bugs or ideas: uggie.the.developer@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen and your moon orbits the nearest planet. Release to fly off along the arc. Catch the next planet's gravity before you fall.

There's no sound

Moonsling follows the ring/silent switch, so that your own music keeps playing. Flip the switch to ring, or turn the volume up.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I reset?

Open Settings (the gear on the title or score screen, or Pause → Settings), tap Reset progress… at the bottom, then tap again to confirm.

Who are the other pilots on the daily board?

Computer-controlled bots, flown on your phone on the same sky as you. There is no online play in version 1.0.

The daily reminder

It's off by default. Turn it on in Settings and you'll get one local reminder a day at 18:00. Turn it off the same way.
